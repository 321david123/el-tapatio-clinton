Nuestra Historia

Two decades of real Mexican cooking in Clinton.

Since 2005, El Tapatio has been a Camanche Avenue fixture — the kind of neighborhood taquería where the chips and salsa are made in-house, the rice gets called "the best I've ever had," and every plate comes out generous and honest.

The reviews put it best: "authentic Mexican from authentic Mexican people." Nearly twenty years in, that's still the whole idea — recipes cooked the way they're meant to be, at prices that keep Clinton coming back, whether it's a weeknight dinner for the family or margaritas by the pitcher with friends.
